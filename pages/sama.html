Listening

Sama

Sama refers to spiritual listening. In some Sufi traditions it includes poetry, music, recitation and devotional performance designed to awaken remembrance and longing.

Listening

Listening

Sama is more than entertainment within its traditional setting; it is framed as attentive hearing shaped by spiritual intention.
