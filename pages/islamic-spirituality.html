Foundations

Islamic Spirituality

Explore spiritual vocabulary within the wider Islamic tradition: worship, intention, remembrance, character, mercy and ihsan.

Ihsan

Worship with awareness.

Islamic spirituality often connects outward worship with inward quality: sincerity, humility, presence and excellence.

Niyyah

Intention before action.

The ethical life turns on intention as well as visible action; spiritual work asks what the heart is seeking beneath the behavior.

Rahma

Mercy as a character.

Mercy is not only a divine attribute in Islamic theology; it is also a human ethical aspiration expressed in conduct.

Dhikr

Remembering the essential.

Remembrance appears across Islamic devotional life, including Qur’anic recitation, prayer, supplication and the repeated mention of God.

A contextual note

Sufi spirituality is best understood within the wider Islamic intellectual and devotional landscape.

This site introduces recurring themes rather than declaring a single doctrine or practice universal across all Muslim communities.
