Remembrance

Zikr / Dhikr

Dhikr means remembrance. In Sufi settings it can refer to silent or vocal remembrance, repeated divine names, Qur’anic passages, prayers and other forms of recollection.

Remembrance

Remembrance

The spiritual aim is to keep awareness of God present in ordinary life rather than confining remembrance to a single moment.

Forms

Forms

Practices vary: individual recitation, group gatherings, breath-linked remembrance, Qur’anic recitation and litanies are found in different traditions.

Traditional understanding

Traditional understanding

Many teachers connect remembrance with softening the heart, strengthening intention and loosening heedlessness.

Approach

Approach

A beginner’s learning is commonly contextual: understand the words, learn the etiquette of the tradition, and practice with attention rather than speed.

In the wider path

Practice is meant to shape attention into character.
