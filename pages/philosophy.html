The inner map

Sufi Philosophy

A guided introduction to the ideas that repeatedly appear across Sufi literature: the heart, the self, remembrance, love, knowledge and ethical transformation.

Qalb

The changing heart

The heart is imagined as capable of forgetfulness and awakening, making spiritual attention a lifelong task.

Nafs

The self that clings

The nafs can refer to the self in different senses; spiritual writing often explores its appetites, habits and tendency toward self-importance.

Ruh

Spirit and life

Ruh is a Qur’anic and theological term with multiple dimensions; Sufi texts frequently use it within a language of inward life.

Ishq

Love as movement

Poets use love as the language of longing, surrender and a reorientation of the self toward the Beloved.

Ma'rifa

Knowing by transformation

Knowledge can be described not only as information but as a form of recognition linked to practice, character and presence.

Ihsan

Beauty in conduct

The spiritual path is repeatedly tied to how one speaks, serves, forgives, earns, gives and responds under pressure.

A useful question

What changes in the person who remembers?

Across many Sufi texts, spiritual language is ultimately tested in character. Attention becomes patience; love becomes service; knowledge becomes humility.
